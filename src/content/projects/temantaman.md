## Overview

TemanTaman is a digital auction platform for ornamental plants, addressing limited market access, high pricing, and restricted reach for plant sellers.

## Features

- Real-time auction bidding system
- Seller dashboard for listing management
- Secure payment integration
- Location-based delivery tracking
