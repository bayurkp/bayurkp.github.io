---
title: '3rd Place of IT Business Development Competition'
organization: 'PNB IT Competition #16 x ECO | Politeknik Negeri Bali'
date: 'Oct 2024'
description: 'Awarded 3rd place for developing TemanTaman, a digital auction platform for ornamental plants.'
---

- Awarded 3rd place for developing TemanTaman, a digital auction platform for ornamental plants, addressing limited market access, high pricing, and restricted reach for plant sellers.
- Validated market demand with 871,027,342 ornamental plants produced in 2023, reflecting a 12% year-over-year growth.
- Designed an efficient auction-based transaction system where sellers list plants, buyers place bids, and the highest offer is accepted before secure delivery is arranged.
- Competed under the theme: "Millennials' Innovation in Renewable Technology as a Pillar for Building the Digital Era with Full Environmental Awareness."
